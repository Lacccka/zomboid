A persistent, server-authoritative Reputation and progression system for Project Zomboid multiplayer servers.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Persistent survivor profiles
[*]Current and lifetime Reputation tracking
[*]Levels, titles, strongest path, and points remaining

[h2]Automatic Reputation[/h2]

[h3]Hunter[/h3]
Production milestones:
[list]
[*]100 tracked kills: +10 Hunter
[*]250 tracked kills: +20 Hunter
[*]500 tracked kills: +40 Hunter
[*]1,000 tracked kills: +75 Hunter
[/list]

Hunter automation includes configurable presets, daily limits, scan intervals, reconnect persistence, and suspicious-jump protection.

[h3]Community - QP Supply Requests[/h3]
When QP Supply Requests is installed, administrators can enable Community Reputation for completed requests.

[list]
[*]Default reward: +5 Community per eligible completed request
[*]Only survivors with a positive final contribution qualify
[*]Self-created requests do not grant automatic Reputation
[*]Shared Community daily cap is configurable
[*]Stable event IDs prevent duplicate rewards after reconnects or restarts
[*]Rewards are fixed per completion and do not scale per item
[/list]

Community automation is disabled by default until an administrator enables it.

QP Survivor Tasks is not an automatic Reputation source. QP Survivor Contracts keeps its separate administrator-configured Primary and Secondary Reputation rewards.

[h2]Administration[/h2]
Open:

View Reputation -> Admin Editor -> Automation Settings

Administrators can configure Hunter and Community automation, reward values, daily caps, and safety controls.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Existing profiles, points, lifetime points, history, Hunter progress, settings, and award ledgers remain compatible. No save reset is required.
