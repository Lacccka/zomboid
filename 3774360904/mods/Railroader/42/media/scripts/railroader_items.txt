    /***********************************************************************
     * Railroader -- RR_EngineerNotebook, "the engineer's notebook".
     *
     * The in-lore manual. A character who took the RAILROADER PROFESSION starts
     * the game carrying his own pocket notebook: eight pages of crib-sheet notes
     * plus a ninth naming the project's supporters,
     * one fact per line, ordered by what the survivor needs first -- where the 800
     * stands and how to board it, cranking it, moving and stopping it, reading the
     * control stand, signals and lights, fuel and battery, switches and impacts,
     * derailment and the cab. See shared/Railroader/RR_Manual.lua.
     *
     * WHY THIS SHAPE (verified in the B42 decompile + vanilla Lua):
     *   * CanBeWrite = true is the whole mechanism. It makes the item a written
     *     notebook: Literature.addPage(i, text) fills a page from Lua, the pages
     *     (customPages) are serialized by Literature.save/load like any handwritten
     *     journal, and ISInventoryPaneContextMenu offers "Read/Write ... note",
     *     which opens the vanilla ISUIWriteJournal. No custom UI, no art.
     *   * PageToWrite is the page count the journal UI pages through when the
     *     notebook is EDITABLE (ISUIWriteJournal:new gets it as numberOfPages).
     *     It MUST be >= the number of pages we prefill, or the later pages are
     *     unreachable. 16 = our 8 + eight blanks, so the survivor can go on
     *     keeping the notebook himself (he needs a pen; that is vanilla's gate).
     *   * We deliberately do NOT setLockedBy(): the notebook is HIS, and a man
     *     writing in his own notebook should not be locked out of it.
     *   * A page is capped at 15 lines by the journal UI and 15*80 chars by the
     *     text box; RR_Manual.MAX_LINES/MAX_COLS keep the authored text inside
     *     that, and the unit tests check every EN and RU page against it.
     *
     * Icon/models are the vanilla notebook's (Icon = Notebook resolves to the
     * vanilla Item_Notebook texture) -- a mod item may reference them, so the
     * easter egg still needs no art of its own.
     ***********************************************************************/
    item RR_EngineerNotebook
    {
        DisplayCategory = Literature,
        ItemType = base:literature,
        Weight = 0.3,
        Icon = Notebook,
        CanBeWrite = true,
        PageToWrite = 16,
        StaticModel = EmptyNotebook,
        WorldStaticModel = EmptyNotebook,
        ReadType = newspaper,
    }

    /***********************************************************************
     * Railroader -- THE TWO TRAINING BOOKS (see shared/Railroader/RR_Training.lua
     * and docs/TRAINING_DESIGN.md). They are the road into the cab for a survivor
     * who did not take the Railroader profession.
     *
     * THEY ARE RECIPE MAGAZINES, NOT SKILL BOOKS, AND THAT DISTINCTION IS THE WHOLE
     * SHAPE OF THIS SCRIPT (owner, 2026-08-09). Vanilla splits its literature in two
     * and the player has learned the split without being told it:
     *   * a SKILL BOOK (SkillTrained + LvlSkillTrained) multiplies XP gain -- you
     *     read it and then go and DO the thing faster. It unlocks nothing.
     *   * a RECIPE MAGAZINE (LearnedRecipes) unlocks a capability outright, the
     *     moment you finish it.
     * Ours unlock a capability, so they are magazines: LearnedRecipes, the
     * RecipeResource category, the onCreateRecipeMagazine hook, and magazine art. A
     * player who saw a skill book here would wait for an XP bar that never moves.
     * Do not add SkillTrained to either of these.
     *
     * WHAT LearnedRecipes BUYS US, all vanilla, all verified in the decompile:
     *   * The grant itself. ISReadABook:complete -> IsoGameCharacter.ReadLiterature
     *     (:4843-4852) walks getLearnedRecipes() and calls learnRecipe on each. The
     *     permit is a raw string in knownRecipes, saved with the character (:5358).
     *     RR_Licence still calls learnRecipe as a backstop, and owns the halo.
     *   * The CHARACTER SHEET. ISLiteratureUI:setLists (:358-380) scans every
     *     literature ITEM SCRIPT, files anything with LearnedRecipes under "Recipe
     *     Books", and puts each recipe id in the "Recipes" tab. So both permits show
     *     up in the player's own record of what he knows, which is where he will
     *     look for them.
     *   * "Show known recipes" on the right-click menu (doLiteratureMenu:1105+).
     * The precedent is exact: vanilla's "Basic Mechanics" is likewise a raw string
     * with no recipe script behind it, granted by MechanicMag1's LearnedRecipes and
     * given a name/icon by hand (Recipes.json + ISLiteratureUI.miscRecipes, which
     * RR_Licence fills in for ours).
     *
     * NO `Tags = base:magazine`, AND THAT IS DELIBERATE. Every vanilla recipe
     * magazine carries it, but the tag is an INGREDIENT: MakeBodyMagazineArmor /
     * MakeForearmMagazineArmor (CraftRecipeKey:397,466) consume tagged magazines as
     * armour padding. Vanilla can afford that because its recipes are duplicated
     * across many magazines; the train-handling book is the ONLY source of its
     * permit in the world, and letting a player quietly shred it into a vest is a
     * way to lose the feature with no message.
     *
     * WHY THE PAGE COUNTS DIFFER, and they are the whole difference in feel.
     * ISReadABook.getDuration spends numPages * MinutesPerPage IN-GAME MINUTES
     * (MinutesPerPage is a sandbox option, default 2.0), scaled by the reader's
     * traits -- Fast Reader 0.7, Slow Reader 1.3, reading glasses 0.9. The sitting
     * 0.9 does NOT apply in the cab: getDuration tests isSitOnGround /
     * isSittingOnFurniture, and our driver is merely setIsResting.
     *   * Model journal: 20 pages -> 40 in-game minutes. It was 5 (the numPages
     *     default you get with NO NumberOfPages at all, Item.java:191 -> -1), i.e.
     *     ten minutes -- a rounding error next to walking out to the locomotive.
     *     Raised 2026-08-09 (owner), to 60 first and then settled at 20: long
     *     enough to be a sitting you notice, short enough that the first-contact
     *     loop in TRAINING_DESIGN section 2.1 still lands. TEN TIMES shorter than
     *     the rulebook, which is the ratio the two icons are drawn to.
     *   * Base manual: 200 pages -> 6 h 40 min in-game.
     * Both are therefore read over several sittings, which is FREE and per
     * character: getDuration reads character:getAlreadyReadPages(fullType) back,
     * start() seeks to it, and IsoGameCharacter.save (:5350) round-trips the
     * progress. No SkillTrained is needed for any of it -- readBooks is keyed by
     * item full type alone. (That resume only exists while NumberOfPages > 0, which
     * is the second reason the journal now declares one.)
     *
     * THE ART ENCODES THAT LENGTH, which is the one thing an icon can honestly say
     * at 32 px:
     *   * PaperReport1 + the ArmorMag7 models (vanilla, from ArmorMag7): loose typed
     *     sheets with two seals -- thin, official, one sitting.
     *   * RR_TrainHandling: OUR OWN 32x32, media/textures/Item_RR_TrainHandling.png,
     *     drawn by tools/make_item_icons.py -- a dark green softbound manual with a
     *     printed title band and a deep cream page block. Vanilla models underneath
     *     (Magazine / MagazineGround), because only the ICON needed replacing.
     * A CUSTOM ICON IS JUST A FILE: Texture.trygetTexture("Item_" + Icon)
     * (InventoryItem:2362) misses the .pack atlases and falls through to
     * media/textures/Item_<Icon>.png via ZomboidFileSystem, which searches the mod's
     * own media dir -- the same route media/textures/prof_railroader.png already
     * takes. No WItem_ sprite is needed while WorldStaticModel names a real model.
     * WHY IT IS OURS AND NOT VANILLA'S: every vanilla recipe-magazine icon carries
     * cover art for its own subject -- an anvil, a welding mask, a horseshoe -- and
     * the least-wrong of them (Magazine_Metalworking1) still put an anvil on a
     * railroad rulebook (owner, 2026-08-09). Ours depicts NOTHING: a title band, a
     * rule and two text lines, so it cannot depict the wrong thing.
     * TWO EARLIER PICKS FOR THE BASE MANUAL WERE WRONG AND BOTH ARE WORTH KEEPING
     * IN MIND. Book_Generic + BookOpenTINT/BookClosedTINT: 50 vanilla users, every
     * one a SkillBook -- chosen while avoiding Book11, which would have collided
     * with only five. Then plain Book + BookClosedGround: 46 users, all ordinary
     * Literature, so not a skill book by the letter -- but still a HARDBACK, and in
     * this game a hardback is what an XP multiplier looks like. The rule that
     * survives both mistakes: match the vanilla item that does the same JOB, not
     * the one that looks nice.
     ***********************************************************************/
    item RR_ModelManual_GP7
    {
        DisplayCategory = RecipeResource,
        ItemType = base:literature,
        Weight = 0.4,
        Icon = PaperReport1,
        BoredomChange = -10,
        StressChange = -5,
        NumberOfPages = 20,
        LearnedRecipes = RR_Operate_GP7,
        Tooltip = Tooltip_item_RR_ModelManual_GP7,
        StaticModel = ArmorMag7,
        WorldStaticModel = ArmorMag7,
        ReadType = newspaper,
        OnCreate = ItemCodeOnCreate.onCreateRecipeMagazine,
    }
